## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a nurturing, trustworthy, and organic educational philosophy for an early childhood academy. It balances professional administrative credibility with playful warmth, domestic comfort, and joyful discovery. 

### Visual Movement & Mood
- **Style Archetype:** Soft Organic Modernism with tactile, high-comfort card layering.
- **Tone:** Caring, safe, transparent, and academically grounded without feeling sterile.
- **Atmosphere:** Natural light, earthy botanicals, friendly rounded geometry, and sunny amber interactive focal points.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Nurturing Foundation:** Clean porcelain and warm buttercream canvases replace cold stark whites, instilling a safe and cozy home-away-from-home feel.
- **Institutional Confidence:** Deep maritime school blue anchors typography, primary headers, and academic credentials, ensuring parents perceive structure, rigor, and safety.
- **Joyful Action:** Amber and solar orange provide energetic contrast for primary actions, invitations, visit requests, and child-centered discovery highlights.
- **Botanical Balance:** Gentle sage and leaf greens evoke nature-based learning, well-being, healthy nutrition, and outdoor play.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around warmth, trust, and natural vitality. Light mode is the default and canonical presentation.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1E56A0`):** Academic Navy. Used for institutional identity, dominant headers, primary iconography, navigation anchors, and status accents requiring trust.
- **Secondary (`#E67E22`):** Sunbeam Amber/Orange. Used for primary CTAs ("Okul Ziyareti Planla", "Bilgi Formu"), interactive badges, highlight borders, and energetic focal spots.
- **Tertiary (`#4E8752`):** Sprout Green. Used for nature, organic diet indicators, botanical leaf accents, eco-learning modules, and success confirmations.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FDFBF7`):** Warm Ivory. The primary canvas backdrop. Paired with elevated card surfaces in pure `#FFFFFF` and subtle tonal tints in `#FFF9F2`.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text is set in deep slate navy (`#0D2440` and `#1A2F48`) rather than pure black to preserve organic softness while maintaining strict WCAG AAA legibility.

## Typography

Typography establishes an inviting balance between child-centered friendliness and administrative clarity.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Modern geometric sans with soft, open terminals that radiate warmth and modern authority without feeling juvenile.
- **Body Text (`Nunito Sans`):** Gentle, humanist character structures with high readability at long reading distances, ideal for curriculum details, parental guides, and daily food menus.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use italicized display quotes (`headline-md`) for institutional vision statements and child-growth mantras ("Her çocuk, doğru destekle kendi ışığını keşfeder.").
- Avoid uppercase tracking on body text. Reserve subtle letter-spacing (0.5px) strictly for micro-labels and step badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by comfortable maximum container widths (1280px) to maintain parental reading comfort.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (24px) gutters, 3rem (48px) canvas margins. Multi-column cards, interactive wheel diagrams, and side-by-side informational panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem (20px) gutters, 2rem (32px) margins. Grids collapse to 2 columns for card grids; schedule tables turn into scrollable surface cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (16px) gutters, 1.25rem (20px) outer margins. Cards convert to single stacked flows or horizontal snap carousels for activity showcases.

### Rhythmic Philosophy
Generous vertical air (`space-xl` between sections) ensures the interface feels calm, orderly, and unhurried—an intentional antidote to chaotic parenting workflows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through pillowy, ambient light rather than harsh structural drop shadows, reinforcing a gentle, welcoming aura.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) with occasional soft beige section alternating fills (`#FFF9F2`).
- **Surface Cards (Level 1):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm, low-opacity ambient diffuse shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(22, 53, 90, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(230, 126, 34, 0.04)`. Outlines are gentle and warm (`1px solid rgba(220, 215, 205, 0.4)`).
- **Interactive Hover & Floating Modals (Level 2):** Elevated interactive state with warm amber/navy displacement: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(13, 59, 102, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(230, 126, 34, 0.08)`.
- **Botanical / Floating Micro-Accents:** Decorative leaf badges and video play triggers utilize high-diffusion soft circular halos for physical tactility.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-friendly, organic geometry characterized by generous curves, pill buttons, and soft card corners (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` equivalents).

### Radius Hierarchy
- **Pill (Fully Rounded):** Primary CTA buttons, category badges, numeric progress steps, and quick-filter chips.
- **Large Curves (`2rem` / `32px`):** Educational feature cards, image frames, and cafeteria/dietary menu blocks.
- **Organic Image Frames:** Children's photos and campus facilities feature curved rectangular frames, occasionally punctuated with gentle organic clip paths or soft arched tops to evoke friendly archways and play spaces.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (Amber Glow):** Full pill shape, vibrant `#E67E22` background, pure white bold text, subtle inner top-light highlight, and soft amber hover bloom. Used for high-intent goals: "Randevu Al", "Okul Ziyareti Planla".
- **Secondary Action (Academic Navy):** Pill shape with `#1E56A0` background, high contrast white text, used for structural actions like "Sanal Tur Başlat".
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Neutral border (`1.5px solid #1E56A0`) with deep navy text and transparent background; fills with soft blue tint on hover.

### Content Cards
- Crisp white background rounded to `1.5rem` or `2rem`.
- Encased in a subtle hairline border with warm beige undertones.
- Card headers pair micro-pill tags with prominent `headline-sm` titles.

### Educational Feature Wheel / Diagram (BEM Model)
- Centered circular hub with soft pastel segmented petals (Social-Emotional, Language, Cognitive, Art & Creativity, Physical Development, Values).
- Each sector employs soft tinted backgrounds paired with deep navy icon pictograms.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Generous touch targets (48px minimum height) with `1rem` rounded corners.
- Warm cream background (`#FFFDF9`) that transitions to clean white with a 2px `#1E56A0` outline and faint amber ring on focus.

### Activity Grid & Gallery Chips
- Soft picture-frame cards featuring rounded photographic thumbnails with centered bold typography underneath ("Piyano", "Resim", "Satranç", "Drama").
- Video play cards feature centered translucent white circular play buttons with soft blur and amber play icon accents.

### Nutrition & Daily Schedule Tables
- Day and meal pill-tags (Pazartesi, Salı, etc.) with rounded list rows.
- Left-aligned bold category badge (Kahvaltı, Öğle Yemeği, İkindi) with subtle green and amber organic ingredient markers.
